About Pigskin Vision

Why I started Pigskin Vision

I've been a football fan for years, but as I tried to understand the game at a deeper level, I noticed that there was a large gap between basic football content and highly technical analytics.

Pigskin Vision is my attempt to help bridge that gap.

The goal is to explain football strategy through mathematics, statistics, visualization and machine learning while keeping the football itself at the center.

What I'm building

Pigskin Vision will combine educational articles with interactive tools that let readers explore the concepts themselves.

As the project grows, more advanced ideas from football analytics and machine learning will become part of those explanations.
