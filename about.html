About Pigskin Vision

Teaching the next generation to watch football smarter.

My Story

Why I started Pigskin Vision

I've been a football fan for years, but as I tried to understand the game at a deeper level, I noticed a gap.

Basic statistics were easy to find. Advanced football analysis existed too. What was harder to find was a place that explained how those ideas actually worked in a way that younger fans could understand and explore for themselves.

Pigskin Vision was created to help fill that gap.

The goal is to connect football strategy with mathematics, statistics, data visualization, and machine learning without losing sight of the game itself.

The Mission

Make advanced football analytics understandable, visual, and useful.

Pigskin Vision is an educational football analytics platform built around explanations, visualizations, and interactive tools.

The goal is not simply to show what a model predicts. It is to explain why the mathematics works, how the data represents football, and what those ideas can teach us about the game.

The Vision

Learn → Explore → Discover

Pigskin Vision will grow alongside new ideas in football analytics, beginning with educational lessons and gradually adding interactive data tools and research-inspired demonstrations.
