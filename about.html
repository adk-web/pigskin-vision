My Story as a Football Analyst

I've been a Seahawks fan for 10 years. I’ve lived through the post-Malcolm Butler interception era, the Geno Smith era, and the dominance of the 2025 Seahawks squad.

But when I tried to go deeper, to examine how elite Russell Wilson was despite having relatively lower statistics than other quarterbacks, or specifically why the Legion of Boom was considered one of the best defenses of all time, I faced an issue. There was no way a young fan like me could learn the game at an advanced level.

That’s when I realized that accessible football analytics could benefit young fans around the country. If more people are educated on football analytics, there will be more opportunities to not only enjoy the game, but to innovate and move the game forward, especially for the next generation of football enjoyers.

The goal of Pigskin Vision is to teach the next generation to watch football smarter with an accessible analytics learning tool. My project accomplishes this through a dual-platform project, combining an informational blog website with an educational video channel.
