About Pigskin Vision

Why I built a platform for learning football through analytics, mathematics, and machine learning.

Why I started Pigskin Vision

I've been a football fan for years, but as I tried to understand the game at a deeper level, I noticed a gap between basic football content and highly technical analysis.

Statistics and advanced analysis were available, but it was much harder to find resources that explained how those ideas actually worked in a way that younger fans could learn from.

Pigskin Vision is my attempt to help bridge that gap.

What Pigskin Vision is

Pigskin Vision is an educational football analytics platform. It connects football strategy with mathematics, statistics, data visualization, and machine learning.

The goal isn't just to show a statistic or model result. I want to explain where the number comes from, what it means on the football field, and how someone can explore the idea themselves.

Where it's going

Pigskin Vision will grow through educational lessons and interactive tools covering topics such as player tracking, spacing, probability, play similarity, clustering, and modern machine learning.

As I learn and work on new football analytics problems, the site will continue turning those ideas into explanations and visualizations that make the game easier to understand.
