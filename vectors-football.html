Mathematics of Football

How Can We Represent Football With Vectors?

Vectors give us a simple way to turn player positions and movement into numbers.

Representing Position

Imagine looking down at a football field. A player's position can be described using an x-coordinate and a y-coordinate.

For example:

position = [30, 20]

The first number represents one direction on the field, while the second number represents the other direction.

Together, the two numbers form a vector.

Representing Movement

Suppose a player starts at [30, 20] and moves to [45, 30].

We can find the change in position by subtracting the starting position from the ending position.

[45, 30] - [30, 20] = [15, 10]

The vector [15, 10] describes how the player's position changed.

This is called a displacement vector.

Adding More Information

Position is not the only information we can represent with a vector.

We could also include information such as speed and direction.

player = [x, y, speed, direction]

Now one vector contains four pieces of information about the player.

Representing football numerically like this gives computers a way to work with what is happening on the field.
